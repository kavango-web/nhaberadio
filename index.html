About Kavango Radio

Rooted in the vibrant town of Maun, Botswana—the gateway to the Okavango Delta—Kavango Radio operates as an independent digital audio broadcast station. We exist to celebrate local sound, bring community stories to life, and stream quality audio worldwide.
